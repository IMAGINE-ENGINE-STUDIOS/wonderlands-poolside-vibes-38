# water-features/

Water feature product imagery — **not pools**. A water feature is an add-on product
(waterfall, fountain, bubbler, sheer descent) that is quoted as its own line item.

## Subfolders

- `waterfalls/` — rock/boulder waterfall products, sold in three tiers with color
  selection (see `mem://products/rocky-water-feature-tiers`).

Anything new (fountains, scuppers, deck jets) gets its own subfolder here with a README.
