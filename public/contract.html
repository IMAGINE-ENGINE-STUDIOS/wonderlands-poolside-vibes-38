ACCORDING TO FLORIDA’S CONSTRUCTION LIEN LAW (SECTIONS 713.001-713.37, FLORIDA STATUTES), THOSE WHO WORK ON YOUR PROPERTY OR PROVIDE MATERIALS AND SERVICES AND ARE NOT PAID IN FULL HAVE A RIGHT TO ENFORCE THEIR CLAIM FOR PAYMENT AGAINST YOUR PROPERTY. THIS CLAIM IS KNOWN AS A CONSTRUCTION LIEN. IF YOUR CONTRACTOR OR A SUBCONTRACTOR FAILS TO PAY SUBCONTRACTORS, SUB-SUBCONTRACTORS, OR MATERIAL SUPPLIERS, THOSE PEOPLE WHO ARE OWED MONEY MAY LOOK TO YOUR PROPERTY FOR PAYMENT, EVEN IF YOU HAVE ALREADY PAID YOUR CONTRACTOR IN FULL. 1. Services to Be Provided

1.1 General Services. Wonderlands World Experience LLC (“Contractor”) shall act as the primary contracting entity and project manager for the Project. Contractor may perform services directly and/or subcontract certain portions of the work as necessary.

1.2 Licensed Subcontractor. Client acknowledges and agrees that Tech Terra Corp, a duly registered and licensed contractor, Florida License No. CGC1537275 (the “Subcontractor”), may be engaged by Contractor to perform any portions of the Project that require a professional or trade license under applicable law. For all portions of the Project that require a license under Chapter 489, Florida Statutes, Subcontractor shall be the contractor of record. The selection, coordination, and supervision of the Subcontractor shall remain the sole responsibility of Contractor.

1.3 No Privity. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create any contractual relationship, privity, or obligation between Client and any subcontractor. All communications, payments, claims, and obligations shall be directed solely to Contractor.

1.4 Design and Permits. Upon execution of this Agreement and receipt of the required deposit, Contractor shall coordinate preparation of plans and submit required permit applications to the applicable authorities. Permit approval timelines are estimates only and subject to governmental processes beyond Contractor’s control.

1.5 Construction Services. Contractor shall furnish or coordinate all labor, materials, tools, and equipment necessary to complete the Project in accordance with approved plans, applicable building codes, and industry standards.

1.6 Site Conditions and Access. Client shall provide uninterrupted access to the property during normal working hours. Any abnormal or unforeseen site conditions — including but not limited to underground obstructions, utilities, water table issues, soil conditions, or environmental factors — shall be the financial responsibility of the Client and may require additional charges, handled as a change order under Section 2.2.

1.7 Environmental and Weather Delays. Project timelines exclude delays caused by weather, unsafe conditions, acts of God, or other circumstances beyond Contractor’s reasonable control.

3. Payment Terms

3.1 Contract Price. The total contract price shall be defined in the approved proposal or scope document. Total contract price: __________.

3.2 Payment Schedule. Payments shall be made in the following fixed installments, unless otherwise agreed in writing:

  • Initial Deposit: Thirty percent (30%) of the total contract price due upon execution of this Agreement.
  • Second Payment: Thirty percent (30%) due upon permit approval and prior to commencement of major construction activities.
  • Third Payment: Thirty percent (30%) due upon substantial progress of construction, as determined by Contractor.
  • Final Payment: Ten percent (10%) due immediately upon final inspection approval or substantial completion, whichever occurs first.

All payments are due the same day invoiced and are a condition precedent to Contractor’s obligation to continue work.

4. Termination

4.1 Termination for Nonpayment. If Client fails to make timely payment, Contractor may immediately suspend work. If payment is not received within ten (10) calendar days of written notice, this Agreement shall be automatically terminated. Upon termination for nonpayment:

  • Contractor shall have no further obligation to perform services.
  • Contractor may retain amounts already paid only to the extent of (a) the value of work performed up to termination, (b) costs incurred up to termination, and (c) damages resulting from the termination as provided in Section 4.2. Any amount paid beyond (a), (b), and (c) shall be refunded to Client within thirty (30) days of termination.
  • Client remains responsible for any work completed and costs incurred up to termination, and for any balance of (a), (b), and (c) not covered by amounts already paid.

4.2 Termination by Client. Client may cancel this Agreement at any time by written notice. Upon cancellation, Client shall pay Contractor: (a) the value of all work performed through the cancellation date; (b) all costs incurred through the cancellation date, including materials ordered, permit and design costs, and subcontractor commitments that cannot reasonably be cancelled; and (c) Contractor’s damages resulting from the cancellation. No fixed cancellation fee or percentage applies. Amounts already paid will be credited against (a), (b), and (c); any excess paid beyond those amounts shall be refunded to Client within thirty (30) days of cancellation, and any shortfall shall be due within ten (10) days of Contractor’s final accounting.

4.3 Effect of Termination. Termination does not waive Contractor’s right to collect outstanding balances, enforce lien rights, or pursue legal remedies.

6. Subcontractor Requirements

Contractor shall require every subcontractor engaged on the Project, including the Subcontractor named in Section 1.2, to:

  • (a) indemnify and hold harmless Contractor and Client from and against claims, damages, and expenses arising out of that subcontractor’s own work;
  • (b) carry commercial general liability insurance and workers’ compensation insurance as required by Florida law, in commercially reasonable limits;
  • (c) name Contractor as an additional insured on its general liability policy and deliver certificates of insurance to Contractor before beginning work; and
  • (d) comply with all terms of this Agreement applicable to its portion of the work.

7. Warranties

  • Workmanship Warranty: Contractor warrants its workmanship for a period of one (1) year from final inspection approval, or such longer period as is stated in the project proposal. During the warranty period, Contractor shall repair or correct any defect in its workmanship at no charge to Client, upon written notice from Client.
  • Manufacturer Warranties: Equipment and materials are subject to manufacturer warranties only, which Contractor shall pass through to Client to the extent assignable.

8. Limitation of Liability

Contractor’s total liability shall not exceed the amount actually paid by Client. The contractor shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

9. Miscellaneous

  • Lien Rights: Contractor reserves all statutory lien rights in the event of nonpayment.
  • Force Majeure: Contractor shall not be liable for delays caused by events beyond its control.
  • Governing Law: Florida law governs this Agreement.
  • Dispute Resolution: In the event of any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the parties agree to first attempt to resolve the matter through good-faith mediation conducted by a Florida Supreme Court certified circuit civil mediator located in the county where the Project is located. Mediation shall be a mandatory condition precedent to litigation. If mediation does not resolve the dispute, either party may file suit exclusively in the state courts of the county where the Project is located, and the parties consent to that venue and jurisdiction. The prevailing party in any litigation arising out of this Agreement shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys’ fees and costs from the non-prevailing party.
  • Notices: All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized courier, or by email with written confirmation of receipt, to the addresses stated at the beginning of this Agreement (or to a replacement address given under this clause).
  • Severability: If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.
  • Assignment: Client may not assign this Agreement without Contractor’s written consent. Contractor may assign this Agreement to a successor of its business without consent, provided the assignee assumes Contractor’s obligations.
  • Waiver: No waiver of any provision of this Agreement is effective unless in writing, and no waiver of one provision or instance waives any other.
  • Electronic Signatures; Counterparts: This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original. Electronic and digital signatures are binding to the same extent as handwritten signatures, as permitted by the Florida Uniform Electronic Transaction Act (Section 668.50, Florida Statutes).
  • Entire Agreement: This Agreement, including Exhibit A, constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements.
